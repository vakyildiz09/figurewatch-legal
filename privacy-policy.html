FigureWatch Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

FigureWatch collects minimal user data. We store your favorites and custom lists locally on your device. We do not collect personal information or track your usage beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

2. How We Use Information

Data is used solely to provide and improve the app experience. Your favorites and lists are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers unless you enable Pro features.

3. Data Sources

We aggregate publicly available information from official government calendars and sources. We do not create or independently verify this information. All schedule data is sourced from official government websites and public announcements.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app connects to external data sources to retrieve public schedule information. Please refer to their respective privacy policies:

  • Official government calendar websites
  • Public press release databases
  • Official organizational websites

5. Push Notifications (Pro Users Only)

If you subscribe to Pro and enable push notifications, we collect and store your device token on our secure servers. This token is used solely to send you schedule update notifications for political figures you have chosen to follow.

We do not use device tokens for any other purpose, including tracking, analytics, or advertising. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device settings or by removing figures from your favorites.

When you unsubscribe from Pro or delete the app, your device token is removed from our servers.

6. Data Storage and Security

Your favorites and preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage mechanisms. For Pro users with push notifications enabled, only your device token is stored on our servers, which are protected by industry-standard security measures.

7. Children's Privacy

FigureWatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Right to Rectification

If you are a political figure featured in this app and believe information about you is inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated, please contact us. We will review your request and update or remove information as appropriate.

You may submit a correction request through the 'Report an Issue' feature in Settings or by contacting us directly at contact.fwatch@gmail.com.

9. Data Retention

Locally stored data (favorites, preferences) remains on your device until you delete the app. Device tokens for push notifications are retained only while you maintain an active Pro subscription and have notifications enabled.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data stored on your device
  • Delete your data by deleting the app
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time
  • Request correction of inaccurate information

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. International Users

FigureWatch is available globally. By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your information (device tokens for Pro users) to our servers, which may be located in different countries.
